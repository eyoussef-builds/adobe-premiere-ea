# Requested vertical excerpts

Build standalone excerpts from approved active footage and the current brief. Preserve the actual voice; do not synthesize missing lines. Exclude abandoned takes unless restoration is authorized. Keep a ledger of original clip IDs, exact source ranges, script, hook and CTA.

Create meaningfully different visual directions when requested: for example a speaker-led treatment, a minimal editorial card treatment and a product-led treatment. Apply the user's current brand only when supplied. No private artwork, fonts, captions or approved prior styles are included.

Use a complete thought and clean original-speed joins. Preserve referents and premises when reordering. A written question can introduce the speaker's real answer, but do not invent a follower or claim a fictional quotation is authentic.

For 9:16 delivery, choose source-aware framing that protects face, hands, microphone and important screen content. Consider a designed landscape inset for screen demonstrations rather than cropping away the explanation. Respect platform UI safe areas.

Word captions need acoustic alignment over the **full selected recording interval**, not a truncated cached excerpt. Check every displayed word against speech, repair uncovered intervals and verify glyph timing in actual decoded frames. A transcript alone does not provide accurate word highlighting. For Arabic use RTL shaping and appropriate line breaking; keep Latin names LTR.

Keep question cards, captions and the face clear of one another. Make type readable on a phone. Motivate camera changes and effects by meaning rather than adding motion at every cut. Add sound only if requested and retain separate voice/effects stems.

Retain editable base sequences and independent style versions. Pre-rendered overlays are permissible only with clear disclosure and retained rebuild resources. Verify native source/timeline mapping and actual native audio, since track routing can change gain or stereo interpretation.

Verify every delivered MP4, not a sample from a large batch: duration, AV decode, timestamps, source mapping, word timing, text layout, audio alignment and opening/closing. Keep a review index with exact filenames/durations and state what was sampled versus fully reviewed. Do not claim all versions are ready until all requested files pass.
